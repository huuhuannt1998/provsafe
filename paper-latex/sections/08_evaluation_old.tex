\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

\subsection{Research Questions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \textbf{RQ1:} Baseline vulnerability to prompt injection (direct/indirect).
  \item \textbf{RQ2:} ASR reduction with \sys{} vs baselines.
  \item \textbf{RQ3:} Utility and friction cost (task success, confirmations, latency).
  \item \textbf{RQ4:} Generalization across models/toolsets.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Attack Suite}
Device-name injection, notification injection, untrusted webpage content, multi-user conflict prompts,
and chaining attacks targeting tool-call sequences.
